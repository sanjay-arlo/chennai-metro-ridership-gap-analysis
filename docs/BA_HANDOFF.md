# BA Handoff — Chennai Metro Ridership Gap

## Objective
Identify station/corridor demand gaps and prioritise last-mile or service interventions.

## Stakeholders
Metro leadership; station operations; planning teams; feeder/last-mile partners; commercial/fare teams; city mobility stakeholders.

## Core requirements
- Compare actual vs projected daily ridership.
- Rank stations and corridors by gap.
- Test last-mile and fare-sensitivity signals.
- Surface actionable growth priorities.
- Clearly label illustrative data.

## Decision rule
Prioritise high projected-demand gaps where connectivity is weak and intervention is controllable. Use as planning evidence, not as official transport forecasts.

## Acceptance criteria
KPIs reconcile to source data; filters update station/corridor views; gap ranking is explainable; dashboard works client-side; disclaimer remains visible.

## Production controls
Verified smart-card data, planning baseline lineage, feeder counts, station master, service/fare history, anomaly checks and refresh governance.
